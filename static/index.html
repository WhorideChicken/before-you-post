          What happens to your photo

          This site stores nothing. In standard mode the photo goes to the Meta Model API, which during the hackathon runs on free credits under which Meta may use submitted content for product improvement: prefer staged photos there, or private mode. The safe copy is a PNG with no EXIF or GPS data. Live runs are rate limited and run one at a time.
